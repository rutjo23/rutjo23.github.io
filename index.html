Corporate voluntary disclosure

I am a PhD student in Accounting Information Systems at Rutgers University. My research focuses on firms’ voluntary disclosure decisions, especially what information managers choose to disclose beyond reporting requirements and how they present it.

I am particularly interested in corporate disclosures about intangible assets and environmental, social, and governance (ESG) matters. I also study corporate reporting as an information system, with an interest in how XBRL tagging affects the comparability and usability of reported information.

My methodological interests include textual analysis and interpretable machine learning. I use textual analysis to measure tone and readability in corporate filings.
