INSIDE THE MODEL

A little math.
A lot of language.

The learned counts come from your Excel-based Python notebook. This page reproduces its multinomial Naive Bayes calculations in JavaScript, without a prediction service.

What the classes mean

Prepared remarks: substantive, named management/IR speakers before the Q&A boundary.
Q&A answers: those speakers responding after a question-like turn from a possible external speaker. These are automatic candidates, not manually verified answers. Management who speak only during Q&A may be omitted.

How a prediction is calculated

P(word | class) = (count + 1) / (class total + vocabulary)

score = log(0.5) + Σ frequency × log P(word | class)

We use equal class priors, add-one smoothing, and English word tokens. Word order is ignored. Unseen words do not affect the score. No recognized words or a tie produces “Unclassified.” Percentages normalize the two scores and are not calibrated confidence.

What needs human review

The original workbook supplies rule-based Prepared/QA sections. The notebook then estimates speaker roles and answer context. Unresolved calls are excluded. Review Selection_Review and correct ModelLabel in the notebook's Excel workflow before treating these as research-grade labels. The displayed metrics are sentence-level and are not independent across sentences from the same call.

This offline file contains learned aggregate word counts only. Full transcripts and individual speaker records are not included.
