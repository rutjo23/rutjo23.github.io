Readability & Forward-Looking Language

Research question

How difficult are financial disclosures to read, and does language about the future differ from the document as a whole? This project examines both linguistic complexity and the wording companies use to discuss expectations.

Approach

The analysis separates text into sentences and identifies potentially forward-looking sentences using modal words and related expressions. It then measures words, sentences, and complex words for the full text and the selected sentences.

Syllable estimates help identify complex vocabulary. The Gunning FOG index combines average sentence length with the share of complex words: longer sentences and more complex vocabulary increase the score.

Results

The project developed feature-extraction functions and saved illustrative examples for readability and forward-looking language. These provide a way to compare the complexity of an entire disclosure with the complexity of its future-oriented statements.

Interpretation

The saved work demonstrates the measurement workflow; it does not establish an aggregate finding across filings. Language rules can also select sentences expressing uncertainty or obligation, and readability scores describe textual complexity rather than directly measuring reader understanding.
